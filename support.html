How to play

Tap dots to draw straight lines from the green dot to the red one. Every coloured circle must be sliced, and your lines may not cross. Try to finish in as few lines as the par. The "How to play" button in the game shows an animated walkthrough.

Restoring your unlock

Bought the full game before, or on another device? Open Lattice, tap Unlock the full game, then Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Refunds
