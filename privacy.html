Lattice

Privacy policy

Lattice by Lone Light Gaming LLC · Last updated 5 October 2026

Lattice is a puzzle game. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not collect your name, email, location or contacts.

What stays on your device

Your puzzle progress, settings (sound, vibration, difficulty) and whether you have unlocked the full game are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The full-game unlock is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple (or Google on Android). We never see your payment details. To confirm and restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives a random ID created by the app, your purchase records, and basic device details such as the operating system version and country. This information is used only to give you the unlock and is not used to identify or track you.

The web version

The free web version at antipastamasta.github.io is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. The page itself stores progress only in your browser.

Children

Lattice is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: see the support page.
